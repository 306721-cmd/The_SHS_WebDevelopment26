Top Gun: Maverick

After more than 30 years of service, Pete “Maverick” Mitchell is still pushing the limits as a top Navy test pilot. Instead of rising through ranks, he chooses to stay in the cockpit — because flying is the only place where he truly feels free.

When Maverick is called back to train a group of elite Top Gun graduates for a dangerous mission, he is forced to face his past — including memories of Goose and the consequences of his choices. Among the new recruits is Rooster, the son of his late best friend, which makes the mission deeply personal and emotionally intense.

The film is known for its real fighter jet footage, intense aerial combat scenes, and emotional storytelling that blends nostalgia with modern action filmmaking.

⭐ Rating: 9/10

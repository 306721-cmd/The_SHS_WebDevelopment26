Ferris Bueller’s Day Off

Ferris Bueller’s Day Off is a comedy film about a clever high school student who skips school and spends an unforgettable day exploring Chicago with his friends.

Ferris manages to outsmart his principal while enjoying freedom, adventure, and proving that life should be enjoyed instead of wasted.

Fun Facts:
• The parade scene was filmed during a real Chicago parade
• Matthew Broderick improvised several lines
• It is one of the most iconic 80s teen comedies
• The quote “Life moves pretty fast” became legendary

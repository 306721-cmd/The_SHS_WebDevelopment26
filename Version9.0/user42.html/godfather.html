The Godfather

The Godfather is a crime drama film directed by Francis Ford Coppola that follows the powerful Corleone mafia family in post-war America. It explores themes of loyalty, power, family, and corruption as control of the empire is passed from father to son.

The story focuses on Michael Corleone, who initially wants nothing to do with the family business, but slowly transforms into a cold and calculated leader. His journey shows how power can change a person over time.

Fun Facts:
• Marlon Brando used cotton in his cheeks to create Vito Corleone’s iconic look
• Many famous quotes from the movie are still used in pop culture today
• The film won the Academy Award for Best Picture
• Real locations in New York were used instead of studio sets for realism
